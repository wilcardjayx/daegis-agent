03 · How it works

Detect. Decide. Revoke. Unattended.

DAegis watches approvals as they land, judges the spender's intent, and — for accounts under its guard — pulls the approval before it can be used. One identity does all of it, and it can do only one dangerous thing.

  1. 01

    Detect

    A poller reads Approval logs from X Layer and flags any that grant an unlimited allowance.

  3. 02

    Decide

    The spender's bytecode and history go to a model. Unlimited alone is never the tell — intent is. It returns a verdict and a reason.

  5. 03

    Revoke

    If the account is guarded and the verdict is malicious, the guardian sets the allowance to zero. The verdict is recorded either way.

The guardian can only revoke.

DAegis holds a guardian role on a GuardedAccount, but that role's entire power is one function: set an approval to zero. It cannot move tokens, change the owner, or reassign itself. The safety isn't a promise — it's the shape of the contract. The key never leaves OKX's TEE.

05 · About DAegis

A guardian that acts, not just an alarm that rings.

Most approval tools scan and then leave you to revoke by hand, or ask for a delegation broad enough to move your funds. DAegis does neither. It watches, it judges intent with a model rather than a blocklist, and where it holds a guardian role it revokes the dangerous approval itself — automatically, within seconds, with a power narrow enough to be safe by construction.

The whole loop runs on a single OKX TEE agentic wallet: it writes the verdicts and it performs the revokes, holding no private key on the machine that runs the agent. What you see on this page is that agent's real work on X Layer testnet — nothing here is staged.
